## What is UPI/Online Payment Fraud?

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud is a form of financial cybercrime where attackers trick individuals into transferring money or sharing sensitive payment credentials — such as UPI PINs, OTPs, or bank details — through deception, social engineering, or fake applications.

---

## Realistic Scenario: The "Wrong Transfer" Scam

**Target:** Rohan, a 19-year-old first-year engineering student living in a hostel.

**Step-by-step breakdown:**

1. **Initial Contact:** Rohan receives a WhatsApp message from an unknown number: *"Hi, I accidentally sent ₹5,000 to your UPI ID. Please return it. I'm in urgent need."*

2. **Building Trust:** The scammer sends a fake screenshot of a "successful transfer" to Rohan's UPI ID, making the story seem credible.

3. **The Request:** The scammer says, *"Just scan this QR code to refund me."* The QR code, however, is a payment request — not a refund link.

4. **The Trap Sprung:** Rohan scans the code, enters his UPI PIN, and unknowingly sends ₹5,000 from his own account. The scammer disappears.

**Who is typically targeted:** Students, elderly users, and first-time digital payment users who are less familiar with how UPI requests vs. payments work.

**Consequences:** Financial loss (often non-recoverable), emotional distress, and in some cases, identity theft if bank details were also shared.
