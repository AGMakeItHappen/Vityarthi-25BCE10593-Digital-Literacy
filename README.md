 Project Overview

This repository is my Digital Literacy Portfolio, created as part of the CSE0001 course at VIT Bhopal University. As a Student Digital Ambassador, I have completed five tasks covering digital awareness, professional online presence, coding platforms, email etiquette, and cybercrime awareness.

 Module Summaries

 Task 1 – Digital Literacy Infographic
Created a one-page infographic using **Canva** covering: what digital literacy means, safe internet practices, professional online presence, and useful digital tools for students. The infographic is designed for sharing with batchmates as a quick visual reference.

 Task 2 – Student Digital Portfolio
Set up professional profiles on **GitHub**, **LinkedIn**, and **Kaggle**. Each platform serves a distinct purpose — GitHub for code and projects, LinkedIn for professional networking, and Kaggle for data science practice and competitions.

 Task 3 – Coding & Collaboration Platforms
Completed a beginner challenge on **HackerRank** (Solve Me First) and built a **Google Form** Digital Literacy Awareness Quiz with 5 questions for batchmates. The form is linked to a Google Sheet for response tracking.

 Task 4 – Email Etiquette
Drafted two professional emails — one requesting a deadline extension and one expressing internship interest. Also created a Social Media Do's and Don'ts checklist with 10 points for responsible online behaviour.

 Task 5 – Cybercrime Awareness
Researched and wrote a case study on **UPI/Online Payment Fraud**, detailing how such scams work and who is targeted. Created a comprehensive 'Stay Safe Online' checklist with 8+ actionable tips for college students in India.
